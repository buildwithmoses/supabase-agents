## Timing
Refunds go back to your original payment method and usually appear in 5 to 10 business days.

## Service fee
Courtline charges a $1.50 service fee per booking. The service fee is not refundable, except when a club closes courts for weather.

## Lesson refunds
Refunds for lessons are decided by the club, not by Courtline.
