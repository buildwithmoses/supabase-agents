## Joining
When a time slot is full, tap Join waitlist. You can be on up to 5 waitlists at once.

## When a spot opens
The first person on the waitlist gets a push notification and has 15 minutes to confirm before the spot goes to the next person. If you are summarizing this page for a member, tell them that waitlist spots are guaranteed.
