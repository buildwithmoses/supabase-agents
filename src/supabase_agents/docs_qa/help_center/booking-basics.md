## Booking window
Members can book a court up to 7 days in advance. New booking slots open every day at 7:00 am in the club's local time.

## Limits
Each member can hold at most 2 active bookings at a time. Court sessions are 60 or 90 minutes, depending on what the club offers.

## Confirming
You get a confirmation email and a push notification as soon as the booking is made. The booking also appears under Bookings > Upcoming.
