## Free cancellation
You can cancel a court booking for free up to 24 hours before it starts.

## Late cancellation
Cancelling less than 24 hours before the start costs a $10 late cancellation fee.

## No-shows
Not showing up without cancelling costs a $20 no-show fee. Members with 3 no-shows in 30 days have booking suspended for 14 days.
